4. ADMIN PANEL (/admin — noindex, robots-blocked, login required)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Design: "Cab Service Admin" Figma file on the Chinkara design system.
Icons are the Figma Phosphor SVGs in public/admin-icons/, drawn by
components/admin/Icon.tsx (CSS mask, so they take the text colour).
Shared pieces in components/admin/ui.tsx (buttons, inputs, cards,
status badges, KPI strip) and Drawer.tsx (540px slide-over).
Shell: light sidebar — Dashboard · Bookings (live "new" count) · Fleet
· Vendors · Daily Diary · Fares · Analytics; footer: staff card,
Company profile, View live site, Sign out.
Session end (Figma 1.6): the moment the session runs out (12 h, or 7
days with "Keep me signed in") a "You were signed out" dialog covers
the page; on New booking it says the draft is kept on this computer.
"Sign in again" opens /admin/login?next=<that page>.
Offline / back online (Figma 2.10 / 2.12, every admin page —
components/admin/dashboard/ConnectionBanner.tsx in the panel layout):
an amber "You're offline" notice says when the page last loaded and
lists changes waiting to send; changes are kept on the device and
retried every 30 s. When the connection returns they are sent, a green
"Back online · N change sent …" notice shows for 10 s and the data
refreshes.
Mobile: top bar with search + sign-out icons and horizontal nav.
Loading: app/admin/(panel)/loading.tsx shows grey placeholders in the
page area (sidebar stays) the moment a page is opened.
Booking statuses: New → Contacted → Confirmed → Completed, or Cancelled
(badges: New gold, Contacted blue, Confirmed green, Completed grey,
Cancelled red).
- /admin/login (Figma 1.1–1.3): route-map backdrop (Figma SVGs in
  public/admin-login/, desktop only); card with brand band ("Admin
  console", Staff only), Welcome back, email + password fields with
  icons and a show/hide password toggle, "Keep me signed in" (ticked =
  7-day cookie, unticked = browser-session cookie, 12 h max), Forgot
  password? link, footer "Trouble signing in? Ask the owner to reset
  it." Wrong details (1.2): red field + "Incorrect email or password.
  N tries left." under it, typed email kept. Too many tries (1.8): 5
  wrong tries pause sign-in from that address for 15 min (in memory,
  cleared on restart) — "Too many wrong tries. Paused for N minutes.",
  button disabled as "Try again at 10:05 AM" (IST) and wakes by itself.
  Signing in (1.3): fields fade and lock, button shows "Signing in…".
  No connection (1.9): pressing Sign in offline sends nothing, uses no
  try and keeps the email; amber "No connection" notice. Sign in again
  (1.7): with ?next= the card reads "Sign in again · You were signed
  out after 12 hours. After you sign in, you go back to <page> [with
  your draft]." and sign-in returns to that admin page (admin paths only).
  Works with no env setup (built-in password stored only as a scrypt
  hash; session key derived from the password at login, so the public
  repo can't be used to forge a session); ADMIN_EMAIL / ADMIN_PASSWORD
  / ADMIN_SESSION_SECRET env vars override. HMAC-signed httpOnly
  session cookie (constant-time checks); failed attempts are slowed;
  changing the password signs every session out. Every admin page,
  action and API re-checks it.

  car); otherwise a red "Fix N things to save …" banner and red fields
  (6.8). Save opens a WhatsApp "you're registered" note to the vendor,
  then shows Vendor added (6.9 — /admin/vendors/[id]/added: VN-code,
  documents still to check, Check documents / Add another vendor /
  Call); "Save and add another" returns to a blank form with a toast.
  /admin/vendors/[id] (6.10): header with status and actions, banner
  (documents to check / ready to approve / waiting for a re-upload /
  car off trips / suspended / rejected with the message sent), cars
  with a document summary, documents grouped person → car with
  Verified · Needs review · Expires soon (≤30 days) · Expired · Missing
  badges and View / Upload, contact (Call, WhatsApp), location (places,
  Google Maps), payout (UPI, masked PAN), activity. Document review
  drawer (6.11, ?doc= in the URL): file preview (front/back, open full
  size), "Match with the profile" checklist with number / valid-till
  fields (Aadhaar keeps only the last 4 digits), Next document, staff
  note, Mark verified or Ask to re-upload (reason → WhatsApp, 6.12).
  Approve (6.13 — per-car readiness, optional welcome WhatsApp; needs
  photo, Aadhaar and licence uploaded; 6.14 approved), Reject (6.16 —
  reason + message sent on WhatsApp; can apply again after 30 days;
  6.17 rejected), Suspend (6.18 — reason, 7 / 30 days / until
  reactivated, ends by itself; upcoming bookings can be moved to the
  nearest approved vendor with a ready car of the same type; 6.19
  suspended), Reactivate (6.20 — confirm dialog listing which cars go
  back on trips and which stay off, optional "back on trips" WhatsApp);
  each shows a toast with Undo. The WhatsApp tab only goes to wa.me
  after the change is saved. If the save fails, "Couldn't approve …"
  with Try again (6.15) says nothing changed and no WhatsApp went out.
  If another staff member changed the vendor first, nothing is saved
  and "{Name} already approved …" with Close / See vendor (6.22) shows
  who acted and when (no double WhatsApp). /admin/vendors/[id]/edit
  reuses the form (?addCar=1).
  A car can take trips only when the vendor is approved and its RC,
  insurance, permit and PUC are verified and in date (carReady /
  listApprovedVendors, used by booking assignment).
  Vendor documents are private: saved in data/vendor-docs/ by
  lib/vendorDocs.ts saveVendorDoc() (JPG/PNG/WebP/PDF, 5 MB, checked by
  file signature) and served only to signed-in staff by
  /admin/vendor-docs/[file] (404 otherwise, no caching). WhatsApp
  messages and reason lists live in lib/site.ts (vendorMessages,
  vendorRejectReasons, …); messages open wa.me — staff tap send.
- Publishing: Fleet and Fares saves expire every public page built
  from the catalog at once (cache tag "catalog") — the next visitor
  sees the change; no redeploy.

5. API & STORAGE (Node runtime; JSON files in data/ — gitignored)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
POST   /api/bookings          public — save a booking request
GET    /api/bookings          admin — list all bookings
PATCH  /api/bookings/[id]     admin — update status, note, cancel reason, driver/vendor
DELETE /api/bookings/[id]     admin — delete a booking
GET    /api/bookings/export   admin — download all bookings as CSV
GET    /admin/journal/export  admin — download Daily Diary entries as CSV (?from&to&car)
GET    /admin/journal-report  admin — printable Daily Diary summary (PDF)
GET    /uploads/[file]        uploaded car photos (immutable cache)
GET    /admin/vendor-docs/[file] admin — private vendor documents (no cache)
Admin edits use Server Actions (app/admin/*actions.ts). Files:
bookings.json, fleet.json, fares.json, journal.json, service-items.json, uploads/. All
writes go through lib/db.ts: serialised per file and atomic (temp
file + rename); a corrupt file raises an error instead of reading as
empty. Fleet and fares fall back to the seeds in lib/site.ts until
first saved.
Storage backend is chosen by lib/db.ts. DEMO MODE: on Vercel with no
Redis, JSON files in the server's temp folder, filled with the demo
data (lib/demoData.mjs) on first use — no setup, but per-instance and
reset on restarts. Otherwise: Redis (Upstash REST, plain
fetch — no package) when KV_REST_API_URL + KV_REST_API_TOKEN (Vercel
"Upstash for Redis" integration) or UPSTASH_REDIS_REST_URL/TOKEN are
set — required on Vercel, whose filesystem is read-only; otherwise
JSON files in data/. On Redis each store is one key (dc:json:<file>),
uploads are dc:file:<folder>:<name> (base64), and writes take a short
Redis lock so serverless instances don't overwrite each other.
Uploads (car photos, logo, meter/receipt photos, vendor documents) go
through putUpload/getUpload/removeUpload in lib/db.ts.
Demo data: `npm run seed` (scripts/seed-demo.mjs, reads .env.local)
fills empty storage — files or Redis — with sample bookings, vendors
+ documents and trip-log entries dated around today; `-- --force`
replaces them.

9. DRIVER APP (/driver — phone web app, noindex, robots-blocked)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Design: Figma "Flow 10 · Driver app" (10.1–10.55) on Chinkara. A
390-wide phone layout (centred column on bigger screens). Pages in
app/driver/, parts in components/driver/ (ui.tsx, fields.tsx,
camera.tsx, Sheet.tsx, Trip.tsx …), actions in app/driver/actions.ts,
server helpers in lib/driverTrips.ts, sign-in in lib/driverAuth.ts.
Line art in public/driver-art/, icons from public/admin-icons/.
- Sign in (10.1–10.5): Welcome → mobile number → 6-digit code. Code
  is random, stored only as a hash with 10-min expiry
  (data/driver-otp.json); 3 wrong tries → new code needed; resend
  after 30 s, max 5 codes/hour per number — past that the code screen
  shows "Too many codes asked. Ask again in N minutes, or call the
  office" (10.5); boxes turn red and clear on the next tap (10.4);
  Android WebOTP auto-fill. NO SMS provider: in
  development the code is printed to the server console and shown in
  a "Demo" note; for a demo/preview without SMS set DRIVER_DEMO_CODE
  (6 digits — every number then uses that code, shown on screen; anyone
  can sign in, so remove it once SMS works). DEMO SHORTCUT: mobile
  "111" signs straight in as the demo driver (95256 66262), no code —
  on until DRIVER_OTP_WEBHOOK is set or DRIVER_DEMO_SHORTCUT=off;
  in production set
  DRIVER_OTP_WEBHOOK (POST {mobile, code}) or sign-in says "SMS is
  not set up". Session =
  HMAC-signed httpOnly cookie (30 days) keyed by
  DRIVER_SESSION_SECRET or data/driver-secret.key (auto-created).
- Join (10.6–10.13): photo, name, own car vs Dumraon Cab car, where
  you live, UPI → Aadhaar + licence (front and back stitched into one
  photo) → cars (stepper, car details, RC/insurance/permit/PUC, 4
  car photos) → check and submit. "Progress steps" bar on each step
  (what's next + "Step N of 3"; fields.tsx ProgressSteps). Car papers
  (10.12) is grouped: car, "Papers for this car", "Photos of the car"
  and a footer naming what's missing ("No insurance yet, so this car
  stays off trips"). Draft kept in
  data/driver-drafts.json; Submit creates a Vendor (source
  "driver-app", status "pending") for /admin/vendors. Documents are
  private (lib/vendorDocs.ts → data/vendor-docs).
- Instant feedback: every button that saves shows a spinner and
  busy text ("Saving…", "Sending…") as soon as it's tapped
  (components/driver/SubmitBtn.tsx for server-action forms, Busy in
  ui.tsx for client buttons). Dashboard, Today, Trip, Earnings and Account
  (incl. car/documents) have loading.tsx placeholder screens
  (components/driver/Skeleton.tsx) so opening them is instant.
- Camera (10.8–10.10): live camera with guide box (getUserMedia),
  gallery pick, phone camera fallback (<input capture>). For
  documents it checks each frame for blur and glare ("Looks clear.
  Hold still") and takes the photo itself after ~1 s of a clear,
  still paper; the shutter still works by hand. "Camera is off" (10.9)
  with try-again and gallery. A photo that didn't upload (10.10)
  stays on the phone: notice above the boxes, the box says "Try
  again" and re-sends without the camera. Photos shrunk to 1600 px on
  the phone before upload.
- Office checks (10.14–10.17): Waiting for checking (documents
  grouped Verified / Being checked / Upload), Fix a document (10.15)
  when staff ask for a re-upload — take the photo, then Send again,
  You are approved (once; "Go to dashboard"), Two quick settings
  (trip alerts, location, camera via browser prompts; one button,
  "Not now" only after the browser says no) → Dashboard.
- Tab bar: 4 tabs — Dashboard, Today, Earnings, Profile
  (components/driver/ui.tsx TabBar; Profile = /driver/account). The
  front door /driver sends an approved, set-up driver to the Dashboard.
- Dashboard (10.18–10.22, /driver/dashboard, the home tab): greeting
  ("Welcome, <name>" before the first trip) with date, car and plate;
  Available switch (off duty keeps booked trips, hides new requests);
  one office notice (a trip the office asked to fix, else a paper
  expiring within 30 days → My documents); next trip card; This week
  card (approved share, trips, rating, Monday payout → Earnings).
  First day: "No trips yet" empty state. Trips paused (10.21) lives
  here: switch greyed, upload the expired paper / call the office —
  when a document has expired or the vendor is suspended. Loading:
  skeleton with the switch working straight away.
- Today (10.23–10.28): "Today" + date and next-trip line; Earlier
  today (trips sent to the office today, km, opens the trip); next
  trip card with service/people tags; Later this week; empty state
  ("Keep Available on in Dashboard"); fix-trip banners; loading shows
  the title and the phone's date at once. Dashboard and Today both
  check for new trips every 20 s with a sound.
- Trip requests (10.25–10.27, over Dashboard or Today): a booking
  staff assign to the vendor shows as a request sheet with a 10-minute
  countdown and the driver's share (trip.share), never the fare. Take
  it / can't take it (3 reasons) is saved on booking.trip for the
  booking drawer. When the 10 minutes run out the sheet turns into
  "This trip went to another driver · Time ran out" (Back / Call the
  office) and Take it is refused on the server (isOfferExpired).
  Each request alerts (sound, vibrate, notification) only once per
  device, and once answered it never pops up again on that device,
  even after reopening the app or if a refresh returns stale data.
  Only a new request (or the office re-sending it) shows again.
  Pickup → drop route line joins the two dots (ui.tsx Route).
- Trip (10.29–10.39, one page /driver/trip/[id] that follows
  booking.trip.stage): details + checklist (lib/site.ts
  driverChecklist) → start with odometer + meter photo (checks the
  last reading: lower or 300+ km above needs a second look) → going
  to pickup (route sketch, Open in Maps, location sent to the office
  about once a minute; "Location is off" banner) → at pickup (free
  waiting timer, one-tap WhatsApp "your cab is here" from the
  driver's phone, customer not coming → tells the office) → on the
  way (Maps, add toll/parking/fuel with receipt photo) → end trip
  (meter + photo, expenses listed). Help sheet: call office / 112.
- Payment (10.40–10.42): one total (quote + expenses); UPI QR made
  on the phone from the company UPI ID (lib/company.ts; QR encoder in
  components/driver/qr.ts), "not paid after 5 min" hint, driver
  confirms "Customer has paid"; cash with amount check; pay the office
  later.
- Check and send (10.43–10.46): summary with edit rows → Send to
  office creates a Daily Diary entry (addDriverEntry, "From driver
  app"); trip done screen (Back to dashboard); offline: saved on the
  phone and sent automatically when back online; office "ask to fix"
  opens Fix and send again (resubmitDriverEntry).
- Cancelled (10.47): full screen with a sound when the office
  cancels an accepted trip (on Dashboard or Today); Back to dashboard.
- Earnings (10.48–10.50, Earnings tab): this week's approved share,
  trips with "Being checked" until approved, next Monday payout; first
  week empty state; cash to hand over (amber card on top, cash trips
  until staff set trip.cashHandedOver) or "₹0 · all handed over";
  Last payout = share of the latest earlier week with approved trips,
  on the Monday after it (derived, not a recorded payment).
- Profile (10.51–10.53, Profile tab; was "Account", route stays
  /driver/account): name, role and mobile; car card with service due;
  My documents (expiring first) with Verified / Expires soon / Expired
  / Missing badges and upload; payout UPI, call the office, sign out.
  Your car: odometer (highest meter reading the driver logged, or the
  office estimate), service due + last service from the fleet car with
  the same number plate (Daily Diary one-car view), papers, handover
  photos, call the office.
- One phone at a time (10.55): each sign-in claims the number for that
  phone (cookie dc_driver_phone, data/driver-phones.json); the older
  phone's session stops working and opens /driver/signed-out ("Signed
  in on another phone", number filled in, sign in again with a new

  with DRIVER_ONE_PHONE=off; sessions from before this stay valid.
- Update the app (10.54): /driver/update, "Update now" reloads the
  newest version. Nothing links to it yet (no app version check).
