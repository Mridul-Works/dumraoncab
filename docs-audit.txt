1. THE VERDICT (AT A GLANCE)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Dumraon Cab Service has a real, working website with a clear
proposition, sensible navigation, genuine fleet photos, and good
technical bones (Next.js, proper robots.txt and XML sitemap, tidy
meta tags). But the site currently undermines its own trust claims:

  - Stats counters render as "0+" (customers, vehicles, years, trips)
  - Testimonials and FAQ sections are empty shells
  - No price appears anywhere on the site
  - Hero and share images are generic stock photos, not their cars
  - Google verification tag is still the literal placeholder
    "your-google-verification-code"

The five fixes that matter most: repair the "0+" counters, publish
real testimonials and FAQs, show indicative pricing, replace stock
photos with their own fleet shots, and finish the local-SEO
groundwork (Search Console verification, structured data, Google
Business Profile).

6. ISSUES FOUND
---------------------------------------------------------------------
[CRITICAL] Stat counters display "0+" everywhere
  Home and About both show 0+ happy customers, 0+ vehicles, 0+
  years, 0+ trips - the count-up animation never fires, so the site
  visibly contradicts its own "1000+ customers, 100+ cars" claims.
  Worse than showing nothing. Fix the trigger or hard-code numbers.

[CRITICAL] No pricing anywhere
  Cab customers in this market compare on price first. "Transparent
  pricing" with zero published rates sends visitors to WhatsApp
  just to ask what a trip costs - many won't bother. Even
  "Dumraon -> Patna Airport from Rs. X" per category would convert.

[CRITICAL] Testimonials section is an empty shell
  A 4.9-star rating is claimed, but the testimonials block contains
  no reviews at all. Social proof is the strongest trust lever for
  a local service business.

[CRITICAL] Google verification tag is a placeholder
  The page ships: meta name="google-site-verification"
  content="your-google-verification-code" - the site was never
  verified in Search Console, so nobody is monitoring how (or
  whether) it appears in Google.

[HIGH] No structured data, no canonical tags
  No LocalBusiness/TaxiService JSON-LD (name, phone, geo, hours,
  rating) and no canonical URLs. For a business found via "cab
  service near me" searches, this is the biggest SEO gap.

[HIGH] Booking page is invisible to crawlers
  /booking ships an empty server response and renders entirely in
  client-side JavaScript - slow first paint on cheap phones and
  nothing for Google to index.

[HIGH] Stock photos where real ones exist
  Homepage hero is a Pexels photo; the social-share (OG) image is
  an Unsplash photo of a generic car - while nine genuine fleet
  photos sit unused in the gallery. Real cars with Bihar plates
  build far more trust than stock imagery.

[HIGH] Empty FAQs and thin services page
  FAQ blocks on Home and Contact render with no questions. The
  services page lists six category names with no descriptions, and
  every card links back to /services itself. Nothing to rank,
  nothing to persuade.

[MEDIUM] Loose ends that read as unfinished
  - Social icons (Facebook, Instagram, YouTube) link nowhere
  - No Google Map embed despite a written address
  - Brand typed lowercase "dumraon" in titles and copy
  - "Popular Destinations" section referenced but never populated
  - Keywords promise Innova Crysta / Fortuner / "luxury" that the
    gallery doesn't show

PHASE 2 - LOCAL SEO: THE REAL GROWTH LEVER (2-4 weeks)
  [ ] Verify Search Console (replace placeholder tag), submit
      the sitemap

      more than from websites)

      hours, rating; add canonical tags and per-page metadata
  [ ] Build one real page per service (hourly, outstation, airport,

      Ara, Varanasi, Gaya - with distance, time, "fares from Rs. X"

  [ ] Server-render the booking page so it indexes and paints fast
      on budget phones
  [ ] Add Hindi - a toggle or Hindi-first copy for local audience

PHASE 3 - CONVERSION & PRODUCT (1-2 months)
  [ ] Publish a rate card (Rs./km per category + hourly package
      prices) or an instant estimate inside the booking widget
  [ ] Sticky mobile call / WhatsApp bar (most visitors are on
      phones and want to talk, not fill forms)
  [ ] WhatsApp booking confirmations with driver name, number,
      and vehicle plate

      "police-verified drivers, GPS tracking" claims concrete
  [ ] Performance pass: serve their own imagery everywhere, drop
      remaining stock-CDN dependencies, trim preloads
